Luke Fitzpatrick

Luke currently attends CU Boulder as a graduate student working towards his MS in Creative Technology and Design and has a BS in Computer Science. Luke has a passion for user experience and human-computer interaction in digital spaces. He believes technology should be thoughtfully designed to make any given task seamless and enjoyable for all backgrounds and abilities. Beyond design, Luke has front-end development experience with companies such as Communify and Festo. In his free time, he enjoys illustration, movies, and coding his own games.
